Dropdown auto-width — proposed behaviour

Dropdowns size their width to the longest option so the full text is readable, down to a floor of the field's current width and up to a cap at 30 characters. Options longer than 30 characters are truncated with an ellipsis and reveal the full text on hover. Applies to every dropdown using this select component.
